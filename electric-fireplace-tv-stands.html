Frequently asked questions

Do electric fireplace TV stands actually heat a room?

Most include a real heater rated for a few hundred square feet, and importantly the heater and flame effect usually run independently — so you can run the flame for ambiance in summer without the heat turned on.

Do these need a special outlet or professional installation?

No — nearly all plug into a standard household outlet and need no installation beyond assembling the furniture itself, unlike a gas or wood-burning fireplace.

Will the heat damage a TV mounted above or on the stand?

Most electric fireplace inserts vent heat forward and upward away from the TV compartment, but check the specific clearance recommendations in the product listing, especially for smaller consoles with less vertical space.
