Frequently asked questions

How comfortable are convertible sofa beds for actual daily sleeping?

It ranges widely by product — for occasional guest use, most work fine; for someone sleeping on it nightly, look specifically for memory foam or thicker mattress inserts, noted where available.

Can I rearrange a modular sectional as my space changes?

Yes — that's the main advantage of modular pieces over a fixed sectional, since individual sections can be rearranged or split apart.

How long does it take to convert a sofa bed?

Most fold out in under a minute without tools, though some heavier convertible designs take a bit more effort — check individual product notes.
