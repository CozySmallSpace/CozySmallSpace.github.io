Best Small Bathroom Storage Ideas for Renters

Updated August 2026 · Storage & Organization

Small bathrooms have the least forgiving layout in the whole apartment — no wall space to spare, and usually a strict no-drilling rule since tile repair isn't something a landlord wants to deal with. These picks all add real storage without a single hole: freestanding, adhesive, or hanging over something that's already there.

1. Livilord 3-Tier Freestanding Over-the-Toilet Storage

Budget pick
  • Mounting: freestanding, straddles the toilet tank — no drilling or wall contact needed
  • Function: 3 open shelves plus 4 hooks for towels or a shower puff
  • Best for: turning empty air above the toilet into the bathroom's biggest storage upgrade
Based on our research The space above a toilet is one of the most consistently wasted spots in a small bathroom — this is usually the single highest-impact purchase on this list because it adds three shelves' worth of storage without touching a single wall.

2. NESOMI Adhesive Shower Caddy, Stainless Steel (7-Pack)

Renter-friendly
  • Mounting: adhesive backing, rustproof stainless steel — no drilling into tile
  • Function: shelf plus hooks for shampoo, soap, and a razor, mountable at any height
  • Best for: getting bottles up off the tub edge or floor without damaging tile grout
Based on our research Adhesive mounts on tile are worth extra care during install — clean and dry the surface fully before sticking, since that's the difference between a shelf that holds for years and one that falls after a week of steam.

3. Clear Stackable Medicine Cabinet Organizer Bins

Cheapest fix
  • Function: divides one cabinet shelf into sections for medication, skincare, and small items
  • Material: clear plastic, stackable, no installation of any kind
  • Best for: a medicine cabinet or under-sink cabinet that's become a pile instead of a shelf
Based on our research This is the cheapest item on the list and the easiest to get wrong by overbuying — measure your actual cabinet shelf depth first, since bins that are slightly too deep won't let the cabinet door close.

4. CANYAVE Over-the-Door Towel Rack, Triple Bar with Hooks

No drilling
  • Mounting: hangs directly over a standard bathroom door — no drilling, no adhesive
  • Function: three bars for towels plus hooks for robes or bags
  • Best for: bathrooms with no towel bar at all, or one that's already full
Based on our research This is the fix worth prioritizing if wet towels are ending up on the floor or over the shower curtain rod — it adds towel capacity using a door that's otherwise doing nothing, and it comes down instantly if you move.

Which one should you get?

If you only buy one thing, make it the over-the-toilet shelf — it's the highest-capacity option and the easiest install on this list. If your tub edge is cluttered with bottles, the adhesive shower caddy solves that directly, just give the adhesive time to cure before loading it up. For a medicine cabinet that's turned into a junk drawer, the stackable bins are the cheapest fix. And if towels never have anywhere to go, the over-the-door rack adds capacity you're not using anywhere else in the room.
