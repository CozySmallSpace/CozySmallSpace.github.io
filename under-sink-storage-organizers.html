Frequently asked questions

How do I measure my cabinet before buying an under-sink organizer?

Measure the width at the narrowest point around your pipes, not just the total cabinet width — that's the number that actually determines whether a rack will fit, and it's why expandable options tend to be the safer choice.

Will an under-sink organizer work with a garbage disposal or filtration system installed?

Most standard racks will still fit, but tight cabinets usually do better with a narrower organizer like the Madesmart pick, which is specifically sized to work around those additional fixtures.

Can I add storage without anything touching the cabinet floor?

Yes — an adhesive-mounted caddy on the inside of the cabinet door adds a second storage layer without taking up any floor space, which is useful if the floor space is already full or oddly shaped.
