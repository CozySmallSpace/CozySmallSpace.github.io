Tips & Guides

Practical advice for making a small space actually work — not just another shopping list.

Picking the right product solves half the problem. The other half is fitting it into a space that has no room to spare and, in most cases, walls you're not allowed to drill into. This page collects the smaller, practical advice that doesn't fit neatly into a single product guide — organized by the same categories as the rest of the site.

Each section below links to a full, in-depth guide. Have a specific question instead? Check our FAQ page.

Storage & organization: measure twice, order once

Vertical storage, careful doorway measuring, and under-used zones like under the bed or sink are the fastest ways to gain real storage. The full breakdown, including which room to tackle first, is in our small-space storage guide.

Lighting: layer it, don't just centralize it

Layering light at different heights, staying under 3000K, and using battery or plug-in fixtures instead of hardwired ones all make a small room feel bigger, not smaller. The full approach is in our small-room lighting guide.

Renter-friendly: protect the deposit, not just the wall

Testing adhesive first, respecting weight limits, and installing tension rods snug are what separate a clean move-out from a deposit dispute. The full guide, including the right way to remove adhesive strips, is in our renter-friendly decorating guide.

Multi-functional furniture: buy for the second job, not just the first

Judge a convertible piece on its second job, not its first, and always measure the space it needs when open. The full guide, including how to match a piece to your actual routine, is in our multi-functional furniture guide.

A quick myth to retire

"Small space" doesn't mean "compromise on comfort." It usually just means being more deliberate about what a piece needs to do, how it needs to fit, and whether it needs to be permanent. Every guide on this site is built around that distinction — what a product actually solves for a small space, specifically, so you can decide if it's the right fit for yours.
