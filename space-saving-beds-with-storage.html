Frequently asked questions

How much storage can I really expect under a storage bed?

It depends on the design — drawer-style beds typically offer the most usable, organized storage (comparable to a small dresser), while simple platform clearance is better for flat items like off-season bedding.

Do storage beds require a box spring?

No — all of these are platform-style designs meant to be used with just a mattress, no box spring needed.

Is assembly difficult for a bed with built-in drawers?

It takes longer than a basic bed frame — budget 1-2 hours and a second person to help with the frame, especially for daybeds with multiple drawers.
