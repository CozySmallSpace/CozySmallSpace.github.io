Frequently asked questions

Will a folding desk feel stable enough to work at?

Stability varies by design — look for wider legs or wall-mounted options for the most stable typing/writing surface; very thin folding tables can wobble under heavy typing.

How much space do I actually need for a wall-mounted fold-away desk?

Typically just the width of the desk itself when open (24-40 inches), and almost nothing when folded flat against the wall — it's one of the most space-efficient options here.

Are standing desks worth it in a small space?

If you're already tight on square footage, a standing desk can double as counter space when not in active use, which is a real advantage over a fixed sitting desk.
