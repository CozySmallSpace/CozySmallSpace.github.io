Frequently asked questions

Can I decorate rental walls without losing my security deposit?

Yes — peel-and-stick wallpaper and damage-free picture hanging strips are both designed to come off cleanly with no nail holes or residue, which is exactly what most move-out inspections check for.

How do I plan a gallery wall without putting extra holes in the wall?

Use a frame set that includes a paper layout template — you tape the template to the wall first, mark where each frame goes, and only then hang the frames, so every hole or hanging strip is used exactly once.

Will peel-and-stick wallpaper damage the paint underneath when I remove it?

It shouldn't, if the wall was properly cleaned before application and the wallpaper is removed slowly and evenly — but it's worth testing a small hidden patch first, since results can vary slightly by wall paint type.
