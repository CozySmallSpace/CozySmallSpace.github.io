Frequently asked questions

Do murphy beds require professional installation?

Most are designed for a confident DIY install with two people and basic tools, though the hardware-only kits assume you're also building or already have a cabinet — those are a bigger project than a finished, ready-to-mount unit.

Can a murphy bed be mounted on any wall?

It needs to be anchored into wall studs to safely support the weight, so check stud spacing before buying — this isn't a project that works with drywall anchors alone.

Do I need a special mattress for a murphy bed?

A standard mattress under about 10–12 inches thick generally works, but foam mattresses fold and stay in place better than thicker hybrid or spring mattresses — check the specific bed's weight and thickness limit before buying a mattress separately.
