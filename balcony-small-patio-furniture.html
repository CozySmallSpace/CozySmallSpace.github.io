Frequently asked questions

Will outdoor furniture hold up through rain and sun exposure?

Look for weather-resistant materials specifically — cast aluminum, treated wicker, or marine-grade fabric hold up much better than untreated wood or basic fabric over a full season outside.

How small a balcony can actually fit a bistro set?

Most 3-piece bistro sets need roughly 3x3 feet of clear floor space — measure your balcony before buying, especially with a round table where chairs need room to pull out.

Should I bring outdoor furniture inside during winter?

It extends the lifespan significantly, especially for cushions and fabric pieces — even weather-resistant furniture lasts longer with some seasonal protection.
