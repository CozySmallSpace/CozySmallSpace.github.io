About CozySmallSpace

Are these real reviews, or paid placements?

They're research-backed comparisons, not paid placements. We select products based on specs, ratings, and buyer feedback on Amazon, and we earn a commission if you buy through our links — that's disclosed on every page. A product being featured doesn't mean a brand paid for the spot.

How do you choose what to recommend?

We compare products within a category by function, build quality signals, and real buyer ratings and review counts, then group them into budget, mid-range, and premium picks so you can match a product to your actual space and priorities instead of just picking the most expensive option.

Do you test every product yourselves?

Our picks are research-based comparisons of specs and real buyer feedback rather than claims of hands-on testing in every apartment layout — we're upfront about that so you know exactly what "based on our research" means on each guide.

Shopping & pricing

Do prices on Amazon match what's shown on CozySmallSpace?

Prices and availability change on Amazon's side independently of our guides, so always check the current price using the "Check price on Amazon" button rather than assuming a price mentioned elsewhere on the site is current.

What if a product sells out or gets discontinued?

We periodically review and update guides when a listed product is no longer available. If you find a dead link or a discontinued item, the fastest way to flag it is through the newsletter signup form or the contact details on our About page.

Using the site

How does the Space Planner work?

It's a short, four-question tool — your room type, budget, priorities, and whether you're allowed to drill — that narrows our catalog down to a shortlist matched to your answers. It's a shopping shortcut based on your stated preferences, not a professional design consultation.

Will these picks work in a rental where I can't drill holes?

Many will — our Renter-Friendly category specifically covers no-drill shelving, adhesive organizers, and tension rods. For anything outside that category, check the product's mounting requirements listed in its "Function" or "Mounting" details before buying.

Can I suggest a product or a topic for a future guide?

Yes — the newsletter signup on any page reaches us directly, and topic or product suggestions are genuinely useful for deciding what to cover next.
