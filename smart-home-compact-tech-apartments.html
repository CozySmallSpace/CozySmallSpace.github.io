Frequently asked questions

Can I add smart home devices to a rental without any wiring?

Yes — smart plugs, smart bulbs, and adhesive LED strip lights all work with your existing outlets and sockets, so there's no wiring, no hub installation, and nothing that needs to be undone when you move out.

Do I need a smart home hub to use a smart plug or smart bulb?

No — the products on this page connect directly to your home WiFi and work with Alexa or Google Home without any separate hub, which keeps the setup to just downloading an app.

What's the easiest first smart home device to buy?

A basic smart plug is usually the easiest starting point — it costs under $20, takes a minute to set up, and instantly adds voice and app control to any lamp or appliance you plug into it.
