Frequently asked questions

How do I add plants to a small apartment without using floor space?

Hang them — macrame plant hangers in a window and adhesive no-drill ceiling hooks elsewhere in the room let you display several plants using only vertical space, with nothing sitting on the floor.

Are self-watering planters actually worth it?

Yes, especially if you travel or forget to water on a schedule — the built-in reservoir and water-level indicator mean you're watering every one to two weeks instead of checking the soil every day.

Can I hang plants from the ceiling in a rental without drilling?

Yes — no-hole adhesive ceiling hooks are rated to hold several pounds each and come off cleanly, so you can hang planters without putting a screw into the ceiling.
