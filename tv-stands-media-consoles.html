Frequently asked questions

What TV size will fit on these stands?

Check the specific weight and width rating for each stand — most on this list support TVs from 45" up to 65", but always confirm against your TV's base width, not just screen size.

Will a wall-mounted floating console actually hold the weight of a TV and components?

Only if mounted into wall studs with the included hardware — floating consoles are not designed for drywall-only mounting, so check stud placement before installing.

Is there enough ventilation for a cable box or gaming console inside a closed cabinet?

Look for stands with open shelving or ventilation cutouts specifically if you're storing heat-generating electronics — solid closed cabinets can trap heat.
