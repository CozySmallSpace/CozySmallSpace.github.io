Best Entryway Storage Benches for Small Apartments

Updated August 2026 · Storage & Organization

A small apartment's entryway is usually just a few square feet by the door — but without somewhere to put shoes, coats, and bags, that little space turns into a permanent pile. A storage bench solves it by combining seating with storage in a single narrow footprint. These four options range from a simple cushioned shoe bench to a full hall tree, so you can match the piece to how much you actually need to store.
