Frequently asked questions

Will a tension rod actually hold heavy curtains without falling?

Yes, as long as it's sized correctly for the width and rated for the curtain weight — the most common cause of a tension rod falling is using one that's too short for the gap, which reduces its grip strength.

Can these be used in a shower instead of a window?

Some can — look for one specifically rated as non-slip or shower-safe, like the ALLZONE rod on this list, since standard indoor tension rods aren't always designed for constant moisture.

Do tension rods leave marks on window frames or walls?

Generally no, since they rely on spring pressure rather than adhesive — but on painted trim, using the rubber end caps as designed (rather than over-tightening) helps avoid any indentation over long-term use.
