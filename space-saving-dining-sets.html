Frequently asked questions

How many people can a small drop-leaf table actually seat?

Most drop-leaf and expandable tables in this list seat 2 people folded down and 4-6 when extended — check the specific dimensions for both states before buying.

Will a wall-mounted folding table feel sturdy enough to eat on?

Look for one with a support leg or bracket system (not just a hinge), which is what keeps it from wobbling under plates and elbows.

Is a bar-height set comfortable for regular meals?

It's more casual than a standard dining height — great for quick meals or entertaining, but some people find it less comfortable for long, relaxed dinners.
