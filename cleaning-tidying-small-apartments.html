Frequently asked questions

What's the best way to do quick cleanups in a small apartment without a full-size vacuum?

A cordless handheld vacuum covers most quick-cleanup needs — crumbs, spills, pet hair — without needing to store or drag out a full-size upright, and a 20V model has enough suction for daily use.

How do I mop floors without a mop and bucket taking over the bathroom?

A spin mop system with a foot-pedal wringer keeps the whole setup to one bucket that folds away in a closet, and the microfiber head is machine-washable so you're not storing a separate bucket of dirty water.

Where should cleaning supplies be stored in an apartment with no utility closet?

A rolling cleaning caddy is a practical substitute for a utility closet — it holds sprays, cloths, and small tools in one bag that tucks into a closet corner or under a sink and rolls out when you need it.
